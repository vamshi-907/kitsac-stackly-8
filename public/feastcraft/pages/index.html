How far in advance should I book?
For weddings, 6–9 months is ideal. Corporate and private events typically 4–6 weeks in advance.
Do you provide staff and rentals?
Yes — chefs, waitstaff, linens, glassware, tableware and décor can all be included.
Can menus be customized for allergies?
Absolutely. We routinely tailor for vegan, gluten-free, halal, kosher and severe allergies.
Is a tasting included?
Complimentary tastings are included with our Diamond and Royal packages.
