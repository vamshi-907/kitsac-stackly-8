Weddings

10 wedding food trends defining 2026.

Chef Marco Alaric Sept 18, 2026 6 min read

Weddings in 2026 are quieter, warmer, and more personal — a return to craft. Couples are trading spectacle for storytelling, and menus are following suit. Here are the ten trends we are seeing across the season.

1. Heritage grazing tables

Long, low tables layered with family-style dishes that draw from the couple's heritage. Guests serve themselves, mingle, and linger.

2. Interactive dessert theatre

Live crêpe stations, torched meringues, dessert carts. Sweetness has become a performance.

3. Micro-plated tastings

Six to eight tiny courses, each with a story. Perfect for intimate weddings under 80 guests.

"The best wedding menus disappear into the evening — you don't remember the dishes, you remember how you felt eating them." — Chef Marco

4. Regional botanicals in cocktails

Sommelier-designed cocktails using foraged, local botanicals.

5. Zero-waste plating

Root-to-stem cooking and compostable plating that guests can actually see.
