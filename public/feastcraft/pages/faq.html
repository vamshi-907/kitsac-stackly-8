How far in advance should I book?
For weddings, 6–9 months is ideal. Corporate and private events typically 4–6 weeks in advance.
Can menus be customized?
Every menu is a starting point. We routinely tailor for allergies, cultural cuisine, and event themes.
Do you provide staff and rentals?
Yes — chefs, waitstaff, linens, glassware, tableware, décor and floral can all be included.
Are tastings included?
Complimentary tastings are included with our Diamond, Royal and Luxury packages.
What food safety certifications do you hold?
ISO 22000 and HACCP-certified production kitchens, with daily microbiological audits.
Are your kitchens allergen-safe?
Yes. We maintain separated prep stations and colour-coded utensils for major allergens.
How is pricing structured?
Per-guest based on the package, with transparent add-ons for bar, décor, live counters and staffing.
What is your cancellation policy?
Deposits are refundable up to 30 days before your event. Full T&Cs are shared with your proposal.
